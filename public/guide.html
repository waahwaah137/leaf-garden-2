B Playing the screen

The camera view is the instrument. There are no on-screen keys — you play the picture itself.

Tap

Plays a single in-scale note at the spot you touch. Pitch & brightness follow the position (per the drag mode); tapping a detected leaf adds a brighter accent.

Drag

Press and move to play a soft, continuous texture that follows the leaf under your finger. The texture depends on the drag mode; it’s always quieter than a tap.

find some green

No green = silence

If the frame has too little green, nothing sounds — by design. Point at a leaf, a bush, a tree and the instrument wakes up.

C DRAG MODE — the 1 · 2 · 3 pill

One tap on the pill switches, live, both how position maps to pitch and which texture a drag makes. All three are in key.

123

Mode 1 — Brush

Left→right = pitch, up→down = brightness. Drag brushes soft plucked grains, like rustling leaves — denser over jagged foliage and on faster swipes.

123

Mode 2 — Wind

Left→right = pitch, up→down = octave. Drag is a pitched, airy “whistling wind”; the colour under your finger shapes how much it sings.

123

Mode 3 — Bow

Up→down = pitch, left→right = brightness. Drag is a soft pad that steps cleanly through the scale; the leaf’s shape morphs its tone.

D Surprise & keep

The fast path to a sound you love, and a way to hold onto it.

Random button

Rolls a fresh, curated sound and applies it live — every roll is pleasant and different. The ring around it is a short cool-down before the next roll. (The app also opens on a random sound each launch.)

Pin (keep)

The small yellow dot below the button pins the current sound to your saved list, with a brief “pinned ✓” confirmation.

pins

pins (in the drawer)

Opens your saved sounds. Tap a row to replay it; the × on a row deletes it. Pins persist on this phone.

E The controls drawer

Tap controls to open the DAW-style drawer — nine dials plus an action row. The downward-arrow notch at the top closes it.

F Leaving & coming back

Privacy first: the camera, sensors and sound run only while ListenGreen is the app you’re looking at.

Close LG

Close LG

A clean exit. Stops the camera, audio and sensors and returns to the Enter cover — from there, swipe away to leave, or Enter to start again.

tap to resume

Auto-pause → tap to resume

Switch apps, lock the phone, or minimise, and everything stops immediately. When you return, a dim screen waits — the camera only turns back on when you tap.
