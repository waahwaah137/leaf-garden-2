How to play 🌿

Point your phone at a plant. Hear it sing.

  1. Tap Start and allow the camera 📷.
  2. Aim the back camera at leaves — every plant's shape and color shift the sound, so no two gardens play the same.
  3. Tap a leaf on screen to pluck it — the music leans toward whatever you touch 👆.
  4. Tap controls for the dials: sound bank, pitch, space, mood.
  5. Hit rec to capture a loop and take it with you 🎙️.

Tips

  • 🎧 Headphones or a speaker make it shine.
  • ☀️ Good light = better leaf-tracking.
  • 📲 Add to your home screen — runs fullscreen & works offline.
